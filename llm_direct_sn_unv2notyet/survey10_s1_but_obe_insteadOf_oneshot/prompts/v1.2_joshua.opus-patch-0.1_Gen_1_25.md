## Rule 1 — Trailing particles belong INSIDE the annotated phrase

A grammatical particle that attaches *backward* to the word before it is part of that word's rendering. The SN goes **after** the particle, not before it.

| Particle | Role | Binds to | Rule |
|----------|------|----------|------|
| 了 | aspect/completion | preceding **verb** | SN goes after 了 |
| 的 | attributive/associative | preceding **modifier** | SN goes after 的 |
| 著 / 着 | continuous aspect | preceding verb | SN goes after it |
| 地 | adverbial marker | preceding modifier | SN goes after it |
| 得 | complement marker | preceding verb | SN goes after it |

Worked examples (these are the correct, non-oscillating forms):
- Verb + aspect: `造了<06213><8799>` ✅ — **NOT** `造<06213><8799>了` ❌
- Modifier + 的: `各種各樣的<03605><09001><04327>昆蟲` ✅ — **NOT** `各種各樣<03605><09001><04327>的昆蟲` ❌
  - Note: `的` binds back to the modifier `各種各樣`, so the SN closes the modifier *after* `的`. The head noun `昆蟲` that follows carries its own separate SN — do not pull `昆蟲`'s SN forward, and do not push the modifier's SN past `昆蟲`.

**Mental check before placing any SN next to a content word:** "Is there a 了/的/著/地/得 immediately after this word?" If yes, the SN goes after *that particle*, full stop.
